# קוצ׳יק וקישמיש — pet timers

A single-page mobile web app (Hebrew, RTL) for tracking two pets:

- **Kochik (קוצ׳יק):** time since last food / pee / poop, plus a daily morning + evening medication checklist (turns red if the morning dose isn't marked by 11:00 or the evening dose by 21:00; resets at midnight).
- **Kochik's poop kind:** her diarrhoea comes and goes, so after a poop (and after the "where?" question) the app asks "איך הוא היה?" — תקין / רך / שלשול, stored as `q: "ok"|"soft"|"dia"` (`POOP`, `PETS.kochik.poopType`, `typeSheet`). Shown on the tile (with an "איזה סוג?" link when missing), in the list and the text export, editable on the entry, and counted under the poop tile in the summary.
- **Kochik's ear gel:** a fourth tile, `gel` — given as needed when she is not eating, roughly once in three days. It is an ordinary point entry, but `slow:true` makes its tile count in days and hours (`wordsLong`) instead of a running clock, with a note of the usual rhythm. Counted in the summary like the other point kinds.
- **Kishmish (קישמיש):** time since last food / pee / poop, plus start/stop timers for sleep, crying and crate (shows the live duration, the last duration, and today's total). These are ongoing states, not moments (`state.kishmish.crate.start` is set while she is inside), and they overlap freely (asleep or crying in the crate). The owner does not want more than this tracked — a puppy-pen timer was added and removed; keep the logging light. A timer can be started in the past from its "הוספה" sheet (`tapDur(p, k, at)`): a start time with no end time means "still going on"; only an explicit end time logs a finished stay. A stay that was closed by mistake can be reopened from its history row ("עדיין נמשך").
- **The routine wheel is derived, display-only** (the owner found a tappable wheel next to tappable tiles confusing — two places logging the same meal or poop — so there is now exactly one place to tap: the tiles). `routine(p)` works out the current step from the log every render; nothing about the step is stored: in the crate → כלוב; out of the crate with nothing logged since → טיול וצרכים; a pee / poop / "לא עשתה" → משחק; a meal or "לא אכלה" → צרכים; a result after that meal → round done, crate next. Only entries since she last left the crate (or since midnight) count; a `brk` entry ("הפסקה בשגרה", the one button left on the wheel card) holds the routine until the next entry. Fixing or deleting an entry therefore moves the wheel by itself. Do not reintroduce stored step state (`cyc` / `hist` may still exist in old data and are ignored).
- **The wheel** (`cycleHTML`): an inline SVG ring of five arrow-shaped segments clockwise from the top with an icon each (`STEP_ICON`); earlier steps tinted, the current one filled (red when the crate stay is over the limit), the next one with a pulsing dashed outline and "← הבא" on its label. The centre shows the current step and how long; a line under the ring says what is next and which tile moves it on.
- **Made for people who are not the owner** (her partner and her mother use it too): the box under the wheel says in plain words what is happening and what to press ("מה עושים עכשיו?"), the tiles that move the routine on carry a "עכשיו בשגרה" tag and outline (`routineTap`), and "איך זה עובד?" opens a five-line explanation (`openHelp`). Keep new routine features explainable in that box.
- **Side column:** alerts, the retry card and the wheel are rendered into `#side`, the tiles and the list into `#main` (both inside `#shell`, which carries the click listener). From 1000px wide `#side` is a sticky column beside the scrolling tiles; on phones it is the top of the page. **The wheel is navigation, not a control:** tapping a step (segment or label, `data-go` → `goSec`) scrolls to that step's group of tiles (`#sec-crate` / `-potty` / `-play` / `-food` / `-rest`) and flashes it; it never changes the step.
- **Kishmish's tiles are grouped under headings by routine step** (`sec` in `render`): 1 כלוב · 2·5 צרכים (pee, poop, the "לא עשתה" row) · 3 משחק וחופש · 4 אוכל ושתייה (food, the "שתתה" row). Sleep and crying are not a step: they are two compact tiles right under the wheel in the side column (`.duo`, `#sec-rest`) — the owner wants them always at hand, since in the crate the puppy is either asleep or crying. The group to fill in now carries the "עכשיו בשגרה" tag and its tiles are outlined.
- **Kishmish's tiles follow the routine's order:** crate, pee, poop, a slim one-button row for "לא עשתה" (`none`; a full tile with its own timer was tried and the owner found it awkward), the play card (`playCardHTML`: חבל / קונג ממולא / משטח ליקוק / אילוף עם אוכל → `logPlay`, logs `{k:"play", y}`; the food kinds in `PLAY_FOOD` also log one meal tagged `y:"play"` per 30 minutes — she is tiny), food (with a "לא אכלה" link → `k:"nofood"`), then sleep and crying.
- **Rounds are a view of the log, not separate data.** `roundStarts()` = every crate entry in the log (finished stays, the running one, retry waits); a round runs from one crate entry to the next and is numbered per day. `groupedHTML()` lists a day's real log entries grouped by round (newest round first, entries in order, running timers as rows) — on Kishmish's main screen ("היום לפי סבבים") and in the summary's day view. Every row opens `openEntrySheet` (time, place, delete; start / end for a finished stay; "עדיין נמשך" to reopen). "הוספת רישום" in the list header adds a missed entry at a chosen time.
- **"ניסיון חוזר" (retry), version 2 (owner's spec, 24.11):** a "לא עשתה" starts a retry **only when she really should need to pee** — `needsPee`: more than `RETRY.fresh` (2 h) since the last pee, or her usual gap says it is time (last pee + 25th percentile of the last week's gaps), or she woke up / left an ordinary crate stay / ate within the last hour with no pee since; or a retry is already on. Otherwise it is only a note in the list (no card, no lock). When it does start one, **the crate entry is written with it** (`pushNone`: crate at t+1 ms, same commit, one undo) — no "נכנסה לכלוב" tap. Only when it is logged while that wait could still be running (within `RETRY.wait`) and there is no other crate stay at or after that time or still running — a "לא עשתה" entered late once left two running crate stays (24.11). The card (`retryHTML`) is **one quiet slim row**, first in `#side`: "לנסות שוב בעוד m:ss" (countdown `RETRY.wait` = 10 min from the crate entry; `tick` re-renders when a `data-until` reaches zero) → "לחצץ" when time is up (highlighted, **no sound, no vibration**) → "כשהיא מתעוררת, ישר לחצץ" if she marked "נרדמה" in the crate → "מה קרה בחצץ?" once she is out; always the two buttons פיפי / לא עשתה and a small × (`rtstop`, sets `x:1`). No subtitle, no water line, no questions. "לא עשתה" on the card = out and straight back in, next attempt, fresh countdown. Play and free time are locked "after a pee" meanwhile. **Assumed sleep applies in a retry wait like in any stay**; the card ignores it (its "asleep" state reads only a sleep she marked — from the assumed row's sheet once the assumption is on). **Never make a derived record depend on how a stay is classified later:** on 24.11 `sleepSpans` skipped retry stays, and a sleep the owner had seen all morning (08:46–09:05, 20 minutes of the day's total) vanished; `sleep.js` has a guard — after a stay ends, its assumed sleep changes only through what she does to that stay (a cry or sleep in it, "לא ישנה", editing it). Which stays *were* retries is still derived (`retryScan` in `derive` → `V[p].retryStays` / `retryNones`, then `isRetryStay`: began within `RETRY.gap` of a calling "לא עשתה", lasted at most `RETRY.max`); they are labelled "ניסיון חוזר" in the list and counted by `retryStats` in the summary (`.rtsum`). The old `r` mark is neither written nor read. (History: v1 had a minutes picker and buzz; then a cue-based card with no clock; the owner then asked for this countdown version.)
- **Reminders and alerts for the puppy** (all derived): on the wheel card — awake for an hour → "לכלוב לישון", three hours since a pee while awake → "להוציא לחצץ"; as red cards above everything (`alerts`) — 3.5 hours without food (hypoglycaemia risk for a one-kilo Yorkie), and "לשקול להתקשר לווטרינר" after 6 hours without a pee while awake or 24 hours without a poop. **The food alert steps back once the owner says she offered and it was refused** (a `nofood` entry after the last meal): for `ALERT.again` (45 min) it is a calm, un-red note ("הצעת לה אוכל ב־… והיא לא רצתה", try again in half an hour, and the signs that mean calling the vet); after that it asks again, remembering the refusal. The red card carries a "הצעתי, לא אכלה" button. The owner found a repeated "feed her now, hypoglycaemia risk" after a refused meal only frightening — keep the wording practical.
- **Water:** a "שתתה" button (`k:"water"`); the retry card shows when she last drank, so a missing pee can be read against it.
- **No accidental doubles:** tapping the same button twice within two minutes asks "כבר נרשם… לרשום שוב?" (`recentDouble` / `askAgain`, `DUP_MS`; entries given an explicit time are not checked). The tests set `DUP_MS = 0` except where they test this.
- **Progress:** the summary shows the share of pees on the gravel out of all pees (`successOf` / `successHTML`) — the day's figure, and one column per day in the week and month views.

- **Crate:** a running timer that ends only when someone marks the exit. **Nothing happens inside the crate** (it is empty — no food, no water): logging a pee / poop / "לא עשתה" / meal / drink at time t while the list says she is in the crate or asleep ends that stay and the sleep at t (`impliesOut`). **"בכלוב" is offered as a place for Kishmish since 25.11** (she pooped in the crate): the pee / poop first ends the stay as usual, and choosing "בכלוב" (in the "where?" sheet or later in the entry's sheet) reopens the stay it ended (`pottyInCrate`; not when a later stay has begun). A sleep it ended stays ended. Shown red, like the floor; listed in the current round, not the previous one. It is also enforced as a fact of the list (`enforce`, run in `norm`, `patch` and `commit`): a sleep that began inside a crate stay cannot run past that stay's end, whichever phone logged what in which order. **Leaving the crate always means awake**: every way of ending the stay goes through `leaveCrate()`, which ends a running sleep at the same moment (only a sleep begun in the last 16 hours). After `CRATE_MAX` (2 hours) the tile and the wheel turn red (`crateOver`, 07:00–22:00 only, so a night in the crate is not an alarm). While she sleeps the wheel shows a moon and "נותנים לה לישון".
- **Take-out reminder:** `pottyDue()` shows "זמן להוציא לצרכים" on the wheel card after waking up, leaving the crate, a meal, or when the pee forecast window opens, until a pee / poop / "לא עשתה" is logged, for at most an hour, never while she is asleep or in the crate.
- **Where (pee / poop):** a log entry can carry `w: "out"|"gravel"|"pad"|"floor"|"crate"` (`WHERE`; `home`, `floor` and `crate` are accidents, shown in red; `balcony` counts like the pad; Kochik's choices are outside / balcony / at home / pad; the choices and their order are per pet in `PETS[p].where` — Kishmish is not vaccinated yet and cannot go outside, so her good place is the gravel on the balcony, with the pad in her pen as the acceptable fallback). Right after logging, a sheet asks where (`whereSheet`; "דילוג" leaves it unmarked and the tile then shows an "איפה?" link); any past entry can be changed from its history sheet. Shown next to the entry and on the tile (floor in red) and counted in the summary tiles (`whereLine`).
- **Today's count by place:** the pee and poop tiles on the main screen show "היום: חצץ 2 · פד 1" (`todayWhere`), best place first, with unmarked ones counted as "לא סומן".
- **Dark colours (20:00 – sunrise, `.night`), redone 24.11 for contrast:** every text / background pair is at least 4.5:1 (most 7+); checked with a WCAG ratio script, not by eye. - **Summary (סיכום):** a second view per pet with day / week / month totals, navigable by date (arrows or a date picker). Week and month show per-day averages and a per-day table; tapping a row opens that day. Weeks start on Sunday. Computed from `log` in `renderSummary()` / `stats()`.
- **Charts (summary view):** `timelineHTML` (day and week modes) draws one bar per day, midnight to midnight, with crate / sleep bands, meal ticks and a dot per pee (round) / poop (square), tappable to fix the entry. `whereBarsHTML` (week and month modes) draws one stacked column per day of pees + poops by place, accidents on the baseline; tapping a day opens it. - **Log as text:** the summary's navigation card has "העתקה" (clipboard) and, where the browser supports it, "שיתוף" (share sheet) — `exportLog` / `logText` produce the shown period's entries as plain Hebrew text with times, a heading per day and a per-day total line, for pasting to the dog trainer. - **Summary filter:** tapping a summary tile sets `sumFilter` to that kind and narrows the timeline, the where chart (pee / poop only), the per-day table and the entry list to it; in week / month views a filtered summary also lists the entries themselves. Tap again or "הצגת הכול" to clear.
- **Pee forecast:** `peeForecast()` = the last pee plus the 25th–75th percentile of the last week's gaps between pees (`peeGaps`, nights excluded, needs 4 gaps). Kishmish's pee tile shows "הפיפי הבא צפוי בין 15:40–16:00", then an amber dot while the window is open and a red one after it (no colours while she is asleep or in the crate). - **Assumed sleep (Kishmish) — the owner's six rules (24.11):** it is **derived, never written**. `sleepSpans(p, log, now)` = the logged `sleep` entries plus assumed pieces (`{assumed:true, stay}`). **Crying splits a crate stay into quiet stretches, and each stretch is judged on its own:** nothing said about it → asleep from `ASSUME_SLEEP` (2 minutes, fixed — not an average) after it begins (= after going in, or after the cry ends), until the next cry or the end of the stay; a sleep she logged in that stretch ("נרדמה", "התעוררה" on an assumed sleep — which writes a real entry from the assumed start — or a corrected time) → her entry is the truth for that stretch and nothing more is assumed in it; "לא ישנה" about it → `nsx: [time inside the stretch, …]` on the crate entry (old `ns:1` = the whole stay). **What she says about one stretch never touches another, and nothing done after a stay ends changes its pieces** (guards in `sleep.js`). Assumed rows in the list carry `data-stay` + `data-t`; `assumeSheet(stayId, t)` corrects or removes that one piece. `derive` fills `V[p].sleep` (`assumed`, `stay`) and `V[p].spans`; every reader of sleep (totals, `stats`, timeline — lighter `guess` band, the list — "משוער" rows, `logText`, wheel, reminders, alerts) reads those. There is no "she usually falls asleep after N minutes" line any more (`typicalSleep` removed); `sinceCrate` still annotates a sleep she logged herself.
- **"לא נרדמה" in one tap (owner's request, 24.11):** while Kishmish is shown asleep, the sleep tile and the wheel's green "ישנה…" line carry a "לא נרדמה" button (`notAsleep`). ## Data

**THE RULE: the saved data is one list of entries and nothing else that matters.** Everything shown — last times, running timers, the medication checklist, rounds, the wheel, charts, forecast — is computed from the list every time. Earlier versions also saved some of those as separate numbers next to the list, and a phone holding older information overwrote them (a tile said "last pee 22 hours ago" while the list had today's entries; the medication checklist could disagree with its own entries). **Do not add saved state that the list could tell you**, and change the list only inside `commit()`.

```js
{ v: 2,
  log: [ { id, p: "kochik"|"kishmish", k, t: ts, end?: ts, w?, y?, by? } ],
  legacy?: { kochik, kishmish } }   // the version-1 pet objects, kept untouched and never read
```

- `k`: `food` `pee` `poop` `gel` `none` (went out, nothing; `x:1` = its retry was dismissed) `water` `play` `nofood` `brk` (break in the routine) `med_am` `med_pm` — moments; `crate` `sleep` `cry` — timers. A crate entry may carry `r` (minutes): a short retry stay. **A timer entry with no `end` is running**; stopping it sets `end`, "עדיין נמשך" removes `end`, deleting it cancels the timer. If two phones start the same stay there are two running entries; `stopDur` keeps the earliest. If two phones end the same timer, `patch()` keeps the earlier end. A crate entry may carry `nsx:[t,…]` ("she did not sleep in the quiet stretch containing t") or the older `ns:1` ("she did not sleep in this stay" — switches assumed sleep off for it). A food entry may carry `m` (`meal` / `bite`): the owner's correction of the automatic meal-or-bite reading.
- `id`: the entry's identity for syncing (`newId()`, assigned in `commit`). Changing an entry's time or place keeps its id, so two phones editing one entry do not duplicate it. Version-1 entries get a deterministic id from their content in `norm()` (`m:pet|kind|t|end`), so every phone arrives at the same one; exact duplicates collapse.
- `w` (place of a pee / poop): `out` `gravel` `balcony` `pad` `home` `floor` `crate`; `q`: what a poop was like (`ok` `soft` `dia`, Kochik only); `y`: kind of play (`rope` `kong` `lick` `train`) or `play` on a meal that came from play; `by`: who logged it. `ts` = epoch milliseconds.
- `derive(state)` → `V[pet]` is what the tiles read: the last time per moment kind, `{start?, lastStart?, lastEnd?}` per timer kind, `meds[day][slot]`. `setState()` is the only place `state` is assigned, and it refreshes `V`. `V` is never saved.
- `norm()` turns anything saved or received into this shape and upgrades version 1 (pet objects → `legacy`, a stored running `start` → an entry). It is idempotent.

**Sync:** localStorage key `pets-timers-v2` holds `{state, base}` in one write (`base` = the last list the server is known to have; the old `pets-timers-v1` keys are left as a backup). `push()` sends `diff(base, state)` — entries added, changed or removed, by id — and applies it with `patch()` to the server's current document inside a Firestore transaction (`ref.merge`), so no phone ever writes its whole copy. `onServer()` rebases unsent local changes on top of what arrives; cache-only snapshots are ignored; `resync()` re-reads on return to the foreground; a failed save stays local and retries; undo reverses only its own change. `salvage()` runs once per device and re-adds this person's recent entries that the server lacks.

**Version lock:** `SCHEMA = 2` is written as `v`. A page that meets a higher `v` on the server stops saving and reloads (`outdated`). The Firestore rule requires `request.resource.data.v == 2` on writes, so a phone still running an old build is refused instead of saving over everyone's data. When the data shape changes: bump `SCHEMA`, teach `norm()` the upgrade, update the rule. The rule has been live since 2026-11-23; it was published through the Firebase Rules API after passing the API's own test cases (allowed / old version / stranger), and copies of the rules before and after are in `~/pets-timers-backups/`.

The log cap is 5000 entries (roughly 400 KB, under Firestore's 1 MiB document limit). If it gets close, move old entries to per-month documents. A local backup of the live document from before the version-2 migration is in `~/pets-timers-backups/` on the owner's Mac (not in the repo — it is private data).

**Sign-in gate:** the app cannot be used until the device is signed in with a Google account that the Firestore rules allow (`setGate` / `allow` / `lock`, full-screen `#gate`). The allowed emails exist only in the rules in the Firebase console — never in this public repo; the app learns the answer from the server (first document read succeeds, or `permission-denied` → "החשבון … לא נמצא ברשימת המורשים" with switch-account and retry). When the Google sign-in does not complete, the lock screen says why in plain Hebrew (`gateError`: window closed early, pop-up blocked, no connection, or the raw code), and warns up front when the page is running inside another app's embedded browser (`IN_APP`, e.g. opened from WhatsApp), where Google refuses to sign in. A device that was allowed before (`pets-timers-v1-authok` in localStorage) opens immediately, so the app still works offline. The header status pill still shows sync state and signs out when tapped.
